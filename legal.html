Privacy policy

In one line

We can't read your conversations, we don't keep them, and we don't know who you are.

What Ghost Chat does not collect

  • No account, phone number, email address or contact list. You never sign up.
  • No message content, files, names or avatars on any server. They exist only in the browsers in the conversation, encrypted end to end while in transit.
  • No cookies, localStorage, IndexedDB or offline cache. Closing the tab removes everything on your side.
  • No analytics, advertising, tracking pixels, fingerprinting or third-party scripts.

What is processed, and by whom

To connect two browsers over the internet, some technical data has to pass through servers. None of it includes the content of your conversation or the secret part of your room code.

  • IP addresses are seen by the site host when it serves the page, by the introduction server when browsers find each other, and by the relay when it is used. On a direct connection, the other person's browser also sees your IP address; choose Maximum privacy to avoid that.
  • The room name (the first five characters of the code) is seen by the introduction server. The relay sees only a one-way hash of the full code that it can't reverse.
  • Timing and sizes of encrypted packets are visible to the relay and to network operators. Sizes are padded; constant-rate mode also hides timing.

Legal basis and retention

Processing is limited to what is technically necessary to deliver the service you request (connecting you to the person you invited). Ghost Chat's relay holds a room only while a connection is open; its code writes no logs and Cloudflare's Workers Logs are switched off for it. Cloudflare still processes connection metadata on its network under its own policy. The site host and introduction server are operated by third parties under their own policies, listed below, and may keep standard server logs.

Your choices and rights

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete on request. Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA; for data held by the third-party services below, contact them directly. You can reduce what any party sees by choosing Maximum privacy, using a VPN or Tor, or hosting your own copy with your own servers.

Children

Ghost Chat is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age for online services where you live), and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone, of any age.

Requests for data

Ghost Chat is built so that there is nothing useful to hand over. We have no message content, no keys, no contact lists and no accounts. The relay keeps no records of past rooms. Requests concerning data held by GitHub, PeerJS or Cloudflare should be sent to those services. We will not add a backdoor, and the open-source code lets anyone check that.
